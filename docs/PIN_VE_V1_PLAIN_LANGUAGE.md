## What this is

Two more undefined words from the frozen calculus get proposed meanings:
RepresentedConjecture (a lineage actually contains a represented problem
and answer, linked by a derivation) and TheoryMediatedCriticism (a variant
is eliminated through a criticism whose discriminator comes from a declared
account, not just from the environment). They feed the frozen row N15,
which says: a structural resemblance between evolution and problem-solving
does not make them the same thing.

## Why the definitions are careful

The whole point of row N15 is that the resemblance must be able to hold
while representation and theory-mediated criticism both fail. So the pins
must not hide either condition inside the resemblance. The record shows
this with two concrete structures: one where the pattern holds but there
is no represented content and no account-driven criticism (all three N15
conditions true), and one identical except that content and criticism are
present. Same pattern, different meanings — so the pins decide nothing.

There is a second trap: TheoryMediatedCriticism also appears, negated, in
a frozen certificate row (V_FALLIBILITY). Pinning it too loosely would make
that row self-contradictory; pinning it too strictly would make the row
meaningless. The chosen reading — criticism must run through a declared
account — keeps both rows coherent.

## What was rejected, and why

For representation: counting behavior as conjecture (would collapse N15),
and requiring full Deutsch-grade epistemic packaging (would make the
denial true everywhere for free). For criticism: counting any selection
pressure (would break V_FALLIBILITY), and requiring a complete critical
package on the lineage side (same over-strength problem). Each rejection
has a stated structural reason; no outcome tests were run (steering
defense: rationale-based only).
