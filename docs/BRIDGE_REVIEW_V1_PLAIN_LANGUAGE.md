# Bridge Review — Plain-Language Companion v1

This is the plain-language companion to BRIDGE_REVIEW_V1.md. If the two
disagree, the official record wins.

## What this is

The last item in the frozen work order: a review of every "bridge" in the
project — the four places where the calculus deliberately connects
separately typed layers (graded B, meaning "project-made connection, not a
source theorem"). Each bridge was checked for four things: does it keep
its grade, do its premises actually suffice, does its conclusion sneak
into its own premises, and could unrelated witnesses be spliced across it.

## What the review found

The variation-to-error-elimination bridge (B1) is adequate for what it
claims — a typed analogy, nothing more — but it currently feeds no report,
which was already known. The realization bridge (B2) had a gap that the
new identity and key-typing work now makes expressible: the "same map"
condition can finally be stated in typed terms. It stays open until a
fixture actually exhibits that shared map. The two physical-episode
bridges (B3, B4) remain open: their adequacy depends on the interface
fixtures IC-SP-001 and IC-SP-002, which have never been run. The scan
found no live circularity anywhere, and splicing resistance is now typed
in principle but untested in fact — that is exactly what those fixtures
are for.

## What did NOT change

No bridge was promoted to a theorem, none was reclassified, and none was
edited. The review also confirmed that none of the eleven records produced
in this phase added a bridge — every addition was a definition or an
acceptance axiom — so the inventory is closed. Readiness counts stand:
0 pinned, 2 partial, 18 open; zero discharged; testing PROHIBITED.
