# Piecemeal Calculus Source Register

## What this register is for

This is the authority ledger for
[PIECEMEAL_PREMISE_CALCULUS.md](PIECEMEAL_PREMISE_CALCULUS.md).
It separates four things that must not be conflated:

- a source definition or theorem;
- a scoped imported result;
- a Poietic bridge used to connect independently typed layers;
- an internal audit rule or countermodel obligation.

The calculus is deliberately conservative: it treats an absent certificate as
**not established**, not as falsity or as evidence that a system is
non-creative.

## Copyright, access, and quotation policy

This repository does **not** copy book chapters or journal PDFs whose
copyright is held by their publishers or authors.  It records stable
bibliographic links, controlled short quotations, and exact
chapter/section anchors.  Open papers are linked to their source documents;
their displayed licence is recorded below.  Before redistributing any
downloaded source, check the licence shown by that source itself.

All quotations below are short excerpts only.  Formulae such as
\(P_1\to TT\to EE\to P_2\) are notation rather than a reproduced passage.

### Access and reuse classification

| Source class | What this register supplies | What it does not supply |

## Citation and licence limits that shape the calculus

1. An open paper's availability does not turn every interpretation into a
   source theorem.  The register keeps direct imports and Poietic bridges
   separate.

2. A copyrighted book is cited by author, title, edition/passage, and
   authorised source page.  This repository supplies no copied chapters.

3. A short quotation is not used as a proof.  The formal rule, source grade,
   scope, and countermodel obligation must carry the argumentative weight.

4. The source documents support **scoped** conclusions.  In particular, they
   do not establish:
   - that all creative agents are whole-agent digital, clonable, or
     self-reproducing;
   - that a finite suite is all possible theories or environments;
   - that selection is represented criticism;
   - that a surviving result confirms an explanation;
   - that an ungrounded abstraction introduces a second ontology.

## How to audit a source claim

For any \(\mathsf P\) or \(\mathsf B\) edge in the calculus:

1. Find its source ID in the authority ledger of the calculus.
2. Read the linked primary source at the listed section/chapter anchor.
3. Check the exact permitted result and the prohibited promotion in this
   register.
4. Check the corresponding support, status, and countermodel row in the
   [verification ledger](PIECEMEAL_CALCULUS_VERIFICATION.md).

If a proposed new rule exceeds the stated source scope, it belongs in a new
versioned bridge or conjecture—not in this frozen source import.
