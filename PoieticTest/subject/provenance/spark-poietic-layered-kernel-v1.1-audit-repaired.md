# The Spark–Poietic Layered Kernel

## Revision 1.1: audit-repaired epistemic constitution over physical realization

### Status

Revision 1.1 repairs the attached revision 1.0 against its consolidated critical assessment while preserving the vertical Spark–Poietic division and the founding non-inductivist authority. It makes no historical claim about earlier trunks, audits, or source migration unless the relevant artifacts are supplied.

This document replaces the former attempt to make Spark and Poietic parallel theories of knowledge. They are not parallel. They are vertically typed.

Spark is the epistemic constitution. It defines the conditions under which an operative change is attributable to a system as its own conjectural growth: construction from what the system already holds and what a target supplies, consequential appraisal, mediated operational award, and promotion through a declared role channel. It also owns explanatory appraisal, problems, tentative refutation, provenance, role revision, generator architecture, and every conditional growth or acquisition result about those objects.

Poietic is the physical realization theory. It defines substrates, attributes, tasks, constructors, possible and impossible transformations, information variables, physical resources, causal histories, boundaries, and the constructor-theoretic predicate of knowledge-bearing retention. It proves conditional realization theorems showing when a physical ecology instantiates Spark records, persistence, transition stages, resource budgets, and boundaries. It does not define good explanation, epistemic refutation, award, or knowledge growth.

The interface is Spark's physical bridge. Spark states the abstract obligations R1–R5 and the vulnerable conjecture that physical explanatory creators admit an appropriate realization. Poietic supplies the typed physical witnesses that discharge R1–R5 for any ecology meeting them. The bridge is therefore neither a reduction of epistemology to physics nor a licence for epistemology to declare physical tasks possible.

The governing statement is:

> **Knowledge grows by re-creation under jeopardy in a resistant physical medium: operative novelty is constructed from what the system holds and what its problems expose, appraised by consequences that can answer back, and promoted only through a criticisable award.**

### 0. Layer contract and deletion discipline

The combined theory has three edge types.

A **Spark edge** connects formal epistemic objects and results. A **Poietic edge** connects physical task, causal, information, resource, and boundary objects. A **realization edge** has the form

```text
Poietic witness package + Spark identification map -> Spark realization claim.
```

Deleting a Spark premise deletes its Spark children even when a physical model remains. Deleting a Poietic witness deletes the corresponding worldly application even when the Spark theorem remains. Deleting an identification map deletes only the cross-layer claims that use it. No decorative dependence is counted.

The jurisdiction rules are strict.

**J1, no upward usurpation.** Poietic may realize an outcome channel, account record, comparison, role gate, or causal predecessor. It may not decide that an account is explanatory, good, problematic, tentatively refuted, promoted, or knowledge-growing. Those are Spark relations.

**J2, no downward decree.** Spark may require a record, resource, resistant outcome, boundary, or persistence path. It may not infer that a physical task is possible, that a substrate is an information medium, that a record can be copied, that a capability can be rebuilt, or that a boundary is closed. Those require Poietic witnesses.

**J3, no predicate collapse.** A Spark-held structure is an operative structure with epistemic provenance. A Poietic knowledge attribute is information whose particular value contributes content-sensitively to its own continued instantiation. Neither predicate entails the other. A transient candidate may be Spark-held without being a self-retaining Poietic knowledge value; a chain letter may be Poietic knowledge while failing every Spark criterion of good explanation.

**J4, no test by fiat.** A Spark appraisal counts as consequential only when its realization reaches a Poietic outcome variable whose value is not fixed solely by the candidate-selection or award policy. Internal proof checking, consistency testing, simulation, games, and external experiments may all qualify when their physical task structure can answer differently from what the policy wants.

**J5, no physical bridge by vocabulary.** Calling a string a record, a process a test, a store persistent, or a border a system boundary supplies no realization. Each cross-layer use must cite the corresponding Poietic witness theorem.

The claim grades are `[background]`, `[axiom]`, `[elementary]`, `[conditional]`, `[imported]`, `[conjecture]`, `[residue]`, `[protocol]`, `[interpretation]`, and `[physical witness]`. Commentary has no inferential authority.

# Book I. Spark

## The epistemic kernel, revision 1.4

### S0. Purpose and scope

Spark describes conjectural operative change. It does not define truth by survival, evidence by probability, or knowledge by an acceptance event. A finite success is a finite event. It may show that an account delivered a declared consequence on a declared probe. It cannot turn repetition into support for a universal explanation. A finite clash may logically defeat a universal demand, but an explanatory account becomes **tentatively refuted** only comparatively, when a currently good rival covers the same or broader explicanda and recovers the predecessor's genuine and apparent successes.

Spark is substrate-independent because its objects are typed by function in an epistemic transition rather than by carrier. A brain, institution, lineage, machine, or artistic practice may realize those functions differently. Whether any one of them does is a bridge question, not a consequence of naming it.

Spark has a **core** and a **universal diagonal extension**. The core uses finite event witnesses, typed accounts, the transition constitution, and the Genesis axiom. The diagonal extension additionally assumes universal execution, specialization, and constructive closure. Results that use those stronger assumptions are explicitly marked. This prevents universal computation from being silently imposed on every physical knowledge-bearing process.

### S1. Declared metatheory and finite types

**B1 (Finite syntax). `[background]`** Let \(\mathbb S\) be the set of finite strings over a fixed finite alphabet. Equality is decidable. There is an injective quotation map \(e\mapsto\ulcorner e\urcorner\) with decidable image, a decidable pairing operation \(\langle x,y\rangle\) with projections, and a size-ordered enumeration of \(\mathbb S\).

**B2 (Finite Spark ancestry). `[background]`** Every completed Spark construction, evaluation, comparison, award, and promotion token has a finite directed acyclic ancestry. Induction on completed Spark ancestry is valid. An open history may contain no final event and need not be physically archived in full. B2 is an abstract Spark condition; it does not assert that a physical causal cone is finite or resource-closed. W4 carries that separate physical burden.

**B3 (Distinct outcome sorts). `[background]`** Let \(\mathbb B=\{0,1\}\) be the Boolean sort. Let \(O\) be a fixed finite observational outcome alphabet with distinct values \(o_0,o_1\). Let the probe set be \(Q=\mathbb N\), with canonical encodings into \(\mathbb S\). Boolean verdicts, structure codes, probes, role tags, explicanda, and observational outcomes are distinct types even when an implementation reuses bit patterns.

A structure \(e\in\mathbb S\) may act as a partial program. Write

\[
\varphi_e(x)\downarrow=y
\]

when it converges to \(y\), and \(\varphi_e(x)\uparrow\) otherwise.

**B4 (Execution-observation coherence). `[background definition]`** Fix a total decidable surjective decoding map \(\delta_O:\mathbb S\to O\). Define \(\operatorname{obs}_b(e,q)=o\) when a budget-\(b\) simulation of \(\varphi_e(\bar q)\) converges to some \(y\) with \(\delta_O(y)=o\), and define it as \(\bot\) when no such convergence has occurred within the budget. Equality of partial execution behaviour entails equality of observational behaviour.

**A1w (Finite witnessability). `[axiom]`** If \(\operatorname{obs}_b(e,q)=o\in O\), the event has a finite witness, and every larger budget preserves the same completed report. Define \(\operatorname{obs}(e,q)=o\) exactly when some finite budget reports \(o\). A timeout is not evidence of non-occurrence unless delay is itself an observed outcome under a declared time-bound demand.

**A1i (Internal auditability). `[optional axiom]`** There is a structure `eval` that returns the encoded budgeted report for \(\langle b,\ulcorner e\urcorner,q\rangle\), and the records needed for a claimed replay remain internally accessible. A1i is required only for results or implementations claiming internal audit, deterministic replay, or system-owned inspection. A1w alone permits a finite event to occur without leaving a permanent internal archive.

### S2. Universal diagonal extension

The following commitments are not part of every Spark realization. They define the universal diagonal extension.

**A2 (Universal execution). `[axiom]`** There is a structure \(u\) such that

\[
\varphi_u(\langle\ulcorner e\urcorner,x\rangle)\simeq\varphi_e(x)
\]

for every \(e,x\in\mathbb S\), where \(\simeq\) is equality of partial results.

**A3 (Specialization). `[axiom]`** There is a total structure `spec` such that

\[
\varphi_{\mathrm{spec}}(\langle\ulcorner e\urcorner,a\rangle)=\ulcorner e_a\urcorner
\]

and

\[
\varphi_{e_a}(x)\simeq\varphi_e(\langle a,x\rangle).
\]

**A4 (Constructive closure). `[axiom schema]`** A structure can enact every finite recipe formed from quotation, pairing and projection, constants, composition, universal execution, specialization, cases on total Boolean structures, finite simulation, dovetailing, and unbounded search for a finite witness. An unbounded search may diverge. Every instance used by a proof is named in that proof.

Define observational equivalence by

\[
e\equiv_{\mathrm{obs}}f
\]

when every probe either diverges for both or converges to the same outcome for both. A property is **extensional** when invariant under \(\equiv_{\mathrm{obs}}\).

### S3. Accounts, explicanda, tests, and status

A **demand** is a pair \(d=(q,C)\), where \(q\in Q\) and \(C\subseteq O\) is decidable. It is contentful when \(C\neq O\). A structure violates \(d\) when \(\operatorname{obs}(e,q)=o\notin C\). A violation is a finite negative event by A1w.

A **finite instrument** is a finite set \(F\) of demands. A computably presented **law-family** \(D=(d_0,d_1,\ldots)\) is an unbounded sequence of demands generated by a total structure. At every finite cut only a finite slice is active.

An **explicandum record** is a finite represented event, regularity, conflict, outcome, or counterfactual case that an account undertakes to explain. A **background package** is the finite set of auxiliary accounts used to derive the consequences tested at the current cut. Background standing is operative reliance, not confirmation.

An **account package** is

\[
A=(e,\Xi,B,F),
\]

where \(e\) is the account structure, \(\Xi\) is its current finite explicanda set, \(B\) is its declared background package, and \(F\) is its active instrument. A test addresses the closed package \((e,\Xi,B,F)\), not \(e\) in isolation. An adverse outcome exposes a defect somewhere in that package. Localisation to one component requires a further account.

Relative to a budget \(b\), an account package is:

**Delivered at \(b\):** every active demand has a witnessed allowed outcome by \(b\).

**Unresolved at \(b\):** neither complete delivery nor a witnessed violation has occurred.

**Violated:** at least one active demand has a witnessed forbidden outcome.

These are consequence statuses, not final epistemic standings. There is no status called confirmed, supported, accepted-as-true, or probably true.

A **logical demand refutation** is the finite defeat of a universal demand by a witnessed counterexample. It must not be confused with the comparative epistemic relation below.

A represented account is currently judged an **explanation** of \(\Xi\) when it supplies a common generative or obstruction structure for the declared actual and counterfactual cases. A lookup table, disconnected list of case clauses, bare negation, or primitive outcome selector does not qualify merely by matching outputs.

Let \(\mathcal V\) be a declared variation chart after notation, carrier relabelling, gauge, and correction-fibre motion have been quotiented. A component is **idle** when removing or varying it changes no declared consequence or counterfactual dependency. Let

\[
\mathfrak D_{\Xi,\mathcal V}(A)
\]

be the family of non-gauge-equivalent variations in the declared chart that continue to account for \(\Xi\). **Exact rigidity** means that this family contains only the identity class. Comparative **hardness to vary** is a declared partial structural comparison of such variation families; it need not be scalar, total, or decidable, and goodness is not defined to be exact rigidity.

Write

\[
\operatorname{Good}_t(A\mid\mathcal V)
\]

only when the current criterion family judges that \(A\) is an explanation of its explicanda, has no idle explanatory component, and is hard to vary while preserving the explanatory work. This is a criticisable verdict in \(C_t\), not a decidable truth predicate.

Current account standing is represented by separate, criticisable predicates rather than a positive evidential scale. An account is **conjectured** when it is held as an account. It is **problematic** at cut \(t\) when the active criteria identify a finite apparent flaw, conflict, inadequacy, failed transport, or uncovered explicandum in its closed package. It is **live** at \(t\) exactly when it is currently held and neither problematic nor tentatively refuted under the active criteria. **Tentative refutation** is the named-rival relation defined below. Historical problematicity and refutation tokens remain in the event history even when current standing is later revised. Live is not a positive evidential grade.

An outcome \(x\) is **expected under an account package** exactly when a contrary outcome, under the declared closed test package, would make that package problematic. Expectation is therefore defined through a counterfactual problem relation, not through theory credence. A **crucial test** is defined only relative to at least two currently good rivals over transported common explicanda and is designed so that each possible outcome makes all but at most one rival problematic.

A **problem** is a first-class record

\[
p=(\mathcal A,\Xi,\omega,\mathcal O),
\]

where \(\mathcal A\) is a finite set of represented accounts or background packages, \(\Xi\) is the affected explicanda, \(\omega\) is a finite witness of an apparent flaw, conflict, inadequacy, failed transport, or uncovered case, and \(\mathcal O\) is the open discharge condition. A raw goal, requested output, or physical task with no represented inadequacy among ideas is a **target** or **task record**, not yet a problem.

A problem record remains in the append-only semantic history after apparent resolution. What changes is whether its obligation is currently discharged. Resolution is therefore reopenable.

For account packages \(A^-\) and \(A^+\), write

\[
A^+\succeq_{\mathrm{succ}}A^-
\]

when \(A^+\) covers the same or a transported broader explicanda set and contains an operative account of the genuine and apparent successes of \(A^-\) on its restricted domain.

Define **tentative refutation by a rival** as

\[
\operatorname{TRef}_t(A^-;A^+)
\]

iff all of the following hold: a finite defect trace makes \(A^-\) problematic; the current criteria do not judge \(A^-\) good on the relevant package; \(A^+\) is currently judged good; and \(A^+\succeq_{\mathrm{succ}}A^-\). A failed test without such a rival makes an account problematic, not tentatively refuted.

**A-NJ (Non-justificationist appraisal grammar). `[axiom]`** Compatible outcomes enter as explicanda or delivery records and create no support increment. Promotion changes operative reliance, not truth status. Tentative refutation is only the two-account relation above. Every standing change is reversible by later criticism even though its historical token is not deleted.

The declared content of an instrument is

\[
\operatorname{cont}(F)=\sum_{(q,C)\in F}|O\setminus C|.
\]

The delivered content at budget \(b\) is

\[
\operatorname{del}_b(e,F)=
\sum_{\substack{(q,C)\in F\\\operatorname{obs}_b(e,q)\in C}}
|O\setminus C|.
\]

These are finite frame instruments, not truth, meaning, credence, or explanatory depth measures. Weakening or deleting demands cannot increase \(\operatorname{cont}\). No unbounded system-level claim may use them without a declared readout rule.

For instruments \(F',F\), write \(F'\preceq F\) when each demand in \(F'\) is obtained by enlarging an allowed set or deleting a demand from \(F\). A strict retreat satisfies \(F'\prec F\) and \(\operatorname{cont}(F')<\operatorname{cont}(F)\).


### S4. The diagonal core

The diagonal results belong only to the universal extension A2–A4.

**L1 (Fixed point). `[elementary from A2–A4]`** For every internal structure \(f\) that is total on quoted structures and maps them to quoted structures, there is a structure \(e^*\) such that

\[
\varphi_{e^*}(x)\simeq\varphi_{f(\ulcorner e^*\urcorner)}(x)
\]

for every input \(x\).

**Proof.** By A4 construct a binary structure \(h\) satisfying

\[
\varphi_h(\langle z,x\rangle)
\simeq
\varphi_u\!\left(\left\langle
\varphi_f\!\left(\varphi_{\mathrm{spec}}(\langle z,z\rangle)\right),x
\right\rangle\right).
\]

Let \(e^*\) be the specialization of \(h\) to its own code:

\[
\ulcorner e^*\urcorner=
\varphi_{\mathrm{spec}}(\langle\ulcorner h\urcorner,\ulcorner h\urcorner\rangle).
\]

Specialization and universality then give the displayed equality. ∎

**L2 (No convergence decider). `[elementary from L1 and A4]`** No total Boolean structure \(k\) decides, for every \(e,x\), whether \(\varphi_e(x)\) converges.

**Proof.** If \(k\) existed, A4 would construct a total code transformer that returns an everywhere-diverging structure when \(k\) predicts self-convergence and an everywhere-converging structure otherwise. Its L1 fixed point contradicts either verdict. ∎

**L3 (No non-trivial extensional decider). `[elementary from B4, L1, and A4]`** Let \(P\subseteq\mathbb S\) be extensional and non-trivial. No total Boolean structure decides membership in \(P\).

**Proof.** Choose \(a\in P\) and \(b\notin P\). A supposed decider would permit a transformer that maps an accepted code to \(b\) and a rejected code to \(a\). A fixed point is observationally equivalent to the account selected against the verdict, contradicting extensionality. ∎

Let \(D_0\) be the law-family whose \(n\)-th demand is \((n,\{o_0\})\). A **conformity announcer** may return \(1\) on \(\ulcorner e\urcorner\), intending to announce that no probe will ever produce \(o_1\).

**L4 (No sound and complete conformity announcer). `[elementary from B4, L1, A1w, and A4]`** No conformity announcer for \(D_0\) is both sound and complete.

**Proof.** Given an announcer \(\alpha\), construct a transformer sending a quoted structure \(z\) to a structure that, on probe \(n\), simulates \(\alpha(z)\) for \(n\) steps, produces \(o_1\) if an announcement appears, and otherwise produces \(o_0\). Let \(e^*\) be its fixed point. If \(\alpha\) announces at stage \(N\), \(e^*\) produces \(o_1\) on every sufficiently large probe, making the announcement unsound. If it never announces, \(e^*\) produces \(o_0\) on every probe, making the announcer incomplete. ∎

The diagonal core establishes a negative-witness asymmetry. A forbidden finite event can occur and be recorded. Universal conformity cannot be converted into a finite, sound, complete positive event. This does not by itself establish explanatory knowledge, good appraisal, fertile conjecture generation, or a physical realization.

**Dependency concentration note.** L1 is the sole fixed-point parent of L2–L4. Its actual downstream theorem cluster is T1–T3, T4(c), T9, and T10. T5 instead depends directly on B1, A1w, and A4, while Proposition 11.1 depends on A4 or on an explicitly held compiler. Deleting L1 therefore collapses the diagonal impossibility cluster but not every universal-extension result. Deleting A2–A4 removes the universal extension while leaving the A5 core intact. This is quarantine, not independent replication of the same theorem.

### S5. The completed system object

A Spark state at cut \(t\) is

\[
\mathcal K_t=(L_t,M_t,C_t,P_t,E_t;\Theta_t),
\qquad
\Theta_t=(\pi_t,\beta_t,\chi_t,\tau_t).
\]

The five roles are typed.

\(L_t\) is the library of operative accounts, background packages, and reusable structures. \(M_t\) is the move-set of constructors and transformations. \(C_t\) is the family of test batteries, explanatory appraisal relations, comparison rules, award relations, and promotion criteria. \(P_t\) is the first-class problem store. \(E_t\) is the operative event-interface role, containing structures that register observations, interpret ingress records, pose probes, express results, or actuate outputs. Raw exogenous records are not role occupants merely because they cross the boundary.

The control tuple is also typed. \(\pi_t\) selects targets and schedules constructions. \(\beta_t\) allocates finite budgets. \(\chi_t\) is a permission vector over promotion channels into \(L,M,C,P,E\). \(\tau_t\) is the append-only **semantic event ledger** containing event identifiers, construction ancestry, evaluation outcomes, comparisons, awards, and promotions. A physical realization need not store every old event internally unless A1i or deterministic replay is claimed; it must physically preserve every record used by a later causal step.

At every finite cut the active state and every completed ancestry are finite. The history may grow without a fixed finite bound.

A full operative transition factors as

```text
select target or problem
-> construct candidate
-> derive and expose consequences
-> register resistant outcomes
-> compare where a refutation claim is made
-> apply an operational award relation
-> promote through a permitted role gate.
```

An exogenous record may enter a typed raw-ingress segment of the event ledger without entering any operative role. An operative interface structure in \(E_t\) may later interpret or use it only through an A5 transition. Apart from the declared initial seed, roles change only through promotion.

The permission closure operator \(\mathfrak C\) acts only on \(\chi\): it opens every promotion channel and leaves all other state components unchanged. Call the resulting condition **permission-openness of all roles**. It is a fact about permission only. It is not reflective role-completeness: S8 additionally requires at least one reachable, non-gauge-trivial promotion path into every role. Permission-openness therefore entails neither constructibility, admissibility, scheduling, nor reachability.

The following relations are distinct:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Perm}_t(r) &:\text{ the channel into role }r\text{ is open},\\
\operatorname{Con}_t(e\mid T) &:\text{ a valid finite trace constructs }e\text{ for target }T,\\
\operatorname{Adm}_t(e,r\mid T) &:\text{ current criteria award operative entry into }r,\\
\operatorname{Sched}_t(e,r\mid T) &:\text{ the policy selects that awarded transition},\\
\operatorname{Reach}(\mathcal K_t,\mathcal K') &:\text{ a finite valid transition path reaches }\mathcal K'.
\end{aligned}
\]

No implication among adjacent relations is valid without extra premises.

### S6. Genesis

**A5 (Genesis, or promotion factorization). `[axiom]`** Every non-seed structure newly promoted into an operative role has a finite promotion certificate with the following faces.

**G1, construction provenance.** A finite construction ancestry builds the candidate from structures already held at the construction cut, declared seed operators, and finite target or environment records causally available at that cut.

**G2, consequential exposure.** A finite evaluation ancestry derives or enacts consequences of the candidate against a selected target. At least one award-relevant verdict depends on an observed consequence and is not exhausted by byte identity with an imported record. In any physical realization, that outcome must be supplied by a resistant medium under J4.

**G3, mediated operational award.** The role-write decision cites the construction and evaluation ancestries, is licensed by a criterion currently in \(C_t\), and uses an open channel in \(\chi_t\). The award changes reliance or control. By A-NJ it does not confer support, acceptance, certainty, or truth.

**G4, no direct answer insertion.** Exogenous syntax may enter the typed raw-ingress segment of the event ledger as data, but no exogenous event directly mutates an operative role. Imported syntax becomes operative only through G1–G3.

The initial seed \(\Sigma_0\) is explicit. Seed occupants have provenance distance zero and carry import debt. If target-specific answers are placed in the seed, no descendant may be credited with creating them.

A5 is independently deniable. A transition system with direct oracle writes, identity-only adoption, unevaluated copying, or promotion by fiat is a formal countermodel. A physical case of explanatory knowledge growth that genuinely requires such a transition would attack A5 or the bridge rather than the diagonal core.

A5 is the constitutional keystone, not a theorem smuggled out of computation. Deleting it removes Spark's attribution of non-seed operative novelty, the provenance results of S6, the growth definitions of S8, and the B-Core application, while leaving the diagonal computability results and all Poietic task results standing. That concentration is explicit and is tested through the A5 and bridge protocols rather than disguised as redundancy.

A structure is **stored** at \(t\) when its code occurs in a storage or event record. It is **held in role \(r\)** when it is a seed occupant of \(r\) or its latest role entry has a valid A5 certificate. Holding is operative standing with provenance. It is not truth and need not imply the Poietic knowledge predicate.

**Proposition S6.1 (Provenance totality). `[elementary from A5 and B2]`** Every non-seed held structure has a finite construction DAG whose leaves are earlier held structures, declared seeds, or finite target and environment records. Its constructor-node count defines a finite provenance distance.

**Proof.** Expand every held non-seed leaf by the certificate of its earlier promotion. Time-directed finite ancestry prevents an infinite descending chain. ∎

**Proposition S6.2 (Storage is not holding). `[elementary from A5]`** Appending a code to storage, copying it, or verifying only its identity cannot by itself make the structure held in an operative role.

**Proof.** Those events supply neither G2 consequential exposure nor G3 mediated award. ∎

A promotion is **target-essential** when an admissible variation of the target transcript, with prior state, seed, and policy held fixed, changes the constructed candidate or award verdict, and no target-independent certificate had already established the same role entry. This semantic counterfactual need not be decidable internally.

Define

\[
\operatorname{REDERIVE}(\mathcal K_t,T,b)
\in
\operatorname{Candidate}(e,\kappa,\varepsilon)
\cup
\operatorname{Problem}(p,\eta)
\cup
\{\bot\}.
\]

A candidate return contains the constructed structure and its construction and evaluation ancestries. A problem return contains a first-class problem record and the finite trace locating the failure or inadequacy. Divergence returns no completed result. REDERIVE never promotes its own candidate; construction, appraisal, award, and constitutional role-write remain separately criticisable.

**Conjecture R (Explanatory reconstruction). `[conjecture]`** Across an open-ended class of explanatory targets, no fixed lossless source-to-account decoder transfers explanatory competence to every receiver without receiver-owned construction and consequential appraisal. A universal fixed decoder that did so would refute Conjecture R. It would not refute Proposition S6.2, because operative entry would still require a transition.

### S7. Immediate non-inductivist consequences

The next three clauses are constitutional unpackings, not independent discoveries. They hold because A-NJ defines no support or acceptance coordinate and because tentative refutation is a two-account relation.

**T0.1 (Compatible evidence supplies no support). `[elementary from A-NJ]`** Appending a compatible outcome may enlarge the explicanda record, discharge a finite demand, or leave standing unchanged. No transition in the status algebra increases theory-level support because no such coordinate or operator exists.

**T0.2 (A lone adverse result is insufficient for tentative refutation). `[elementary from the definition of TRef]`** A witnessed violation makes the closed account package problematic. Without a currently good rival satisfying the successor relation, \(\operatorname{TRef}\) does not follow.

**T0.3 (Promotion is not acceptance). `[elementary from A5 G3 and A-NJ]`** An operational award can alter which account, method, criterion, problem, or interface is used. It cannot create a terminal positive epistemic status.

**T1 (Negative-witness asymmetry). `[elementary from A1w, A2, A4, L2, and L4]`** A witnessed violation has a finite certificate, and dovetailing finds every violation that occurs in the universal extension. No total procedure decides in general whether a violation will ever occur, and \(D_0\) has no sound and complete conformity announcer.

A logical universal demand may therefore be refuted by one counterexample event. The epistemic standing of an explanatory account still obeys T0.2.

**T2 (No enumerated universal innocence). `[elementary from T1 and L4]`** No structure enumerates exactly the accounts that will never violate \(D_0\).

**Proof.** Violators are enumerable by finite witnesses. If non-violators were also enumerable, parallel enumeration would decide the partition and yield a sound complete conformity procedure. ∎

**T3 (No complete appraisal by code inspection). `[elementary from L3]`** No total structure decides a non-trivial extensional success property for every candidate from its code.

Restricted proofs, types, static analyses, certificates, and heuristic critics remain possible. No one of them is total, sound, and complete for every candidate in a universal behavioural class.

**T4 (Reach and invariants). `[elementary, with clause (c) from A2–A4 and L2]`** For a repertoire \(\Gamma=(L,M)\):

(a) \(e\notin\operatorname{span}(\Gamma)\) iff some set contains \(L\), is closed under every move in \(M\), and excludes \(e\).

(b) Span membership has finite construction certificates and is enumerable when \(L\) and \(M\) are effectively presented.

(c) Some effectively presented finite repertoires have undecidable span membership.

Clause (c) follows by including a universal simulator move that emits a distinguished structure exactly when a selected computation converges. Openness of the method role is neither necessary nor sufficient for universal reach; reach is exactly the current span.

**T5 (Crucial probes for total rivals). `[elementary from B1, A1w, and A4]`** If two total accounts are observationally inequivalent, a dovetailing comparator eventually finds a probe on which they differ. For partial accounts, a finite distinguishing event is required; divergence alone is not a finite certificate.

**T6 (Finite-sample underdetermination). `[elementary]`** If a finite sample is compatible with two gauge-inequivalent sources in a reconstruction class, no deterministic decoder whose entire source information is that sample can be guaranteed to reconstruct both sources correctly up to gauge.

**T7 (Retreat dynamics). `[conditional]`** Consider a finite active instrument under fair probing, a finite reachable repair graph, recurrent repair-triggering violations, a strict retreat available from every non-minimal survivor, and a policy that always selects a lowest-cost admissible repair while every strict retreat is cheaper than every content-preserving repair. Under a retreat-permitting award rule, each such repair strictly lowers \(\operatorname{cont}(F)\) until a retreat-minimal survivor is reached. An award rule forbidding content-decreasing promotions closes that descent channel.

**Proof.** \(\operatorname{cont}(F)\) is a non-negative integer. The hypotheses force strict descent at every repair-triggering step, so finite descent terminates. ∎

**T8 (Guarded rescue characterization). `[elementary]`** If an award rule admits exactly revisions that restore delivery and do not decrease declared content, its admissible rescues are exactly the success-restoring, content-nondecreasing revisions. Regeneration is one execution-native instance, not the unique one.

**T9 (Permanent proxy gap). `[elementary from L3]`** No total decidable test is complete for a non-trivial extensional conformity property. Every total decidable sound proxy accepts an enumerable strict subset of the conformers.

**T10 (Conditional attainability, permanent uncertifiability). `[elementary from B1, A4, and L4]`** If a move-set enumerates every structure and a conformer exists, dovetailing eventually emits one. ### S8. Generator architecture

A target source is **evaluated** when it supplies finite demands whose outcomes are generated by a resistant medium rather than set solely by the candidate-selection or award policy. Resistance is an abstract interface condition in Spark. Book II supplies its physical realization through task structure, outcome variables, and boundary interventions.

A target source is **owned** at a declared boundary when the system can retain relevant traces, turn them into later targets or problems, and revise the structures that select among them without an external target-author supplying each next problem. Ownership need not be conscious or centrally represented.

At cut \(t\), let the active repertoire be

\[
\Gamma_t=(L_t,M_t).
\]

Its **pure span** is the least set containing \(L_t\) and closed under the constructors in \(M_t\), with no new target record admitted as a leaf. For a finite transcript \(h\), \(\Gamma_t[h]\) is the contextual repertoire obtained by adjoining the records in \(h\). Contextual assembly distance is the minimum construction length from that context. Proposition S6.1 gives a finite historical contextual distance for every non-seed held structure; it does not imply membership in the pure span.

Difficulty is indexical. A structure can have distance zero in one repertoire and lie outside another's span. No repertoire-free assembly difficulty follows from finite syntax.

For a finite instrument \(F\), budget \(b\), repertoire \(\Gamma\), and transcript \(h\), define

\[
\operatorname{Sol}_b(F,\Gamma;h)
=
\{e\in\operatorname{span}(\Gamma[h]):e\text{ delivers }F\text{ by }b\}/\!\approx,
\]

where \(\approx\) is a declared gauge no coarser than observational equivalence on the relevant probes.

A local edit graph supplies a perturbation instrument. **Local delivery rigidity** is the declared fraction or order of gauge-inequivalent neighbouring edits that cease to deliver the active explanatory work. It is local, repertoire-relative, instrument-relative, and budget-relative. A finite sample **pins** a source relative to a reconstruction class when every matching reconstruction is gauge-equivalent to it. Rigidity and pinning are different objects.

**Conjecture C1 (Assembly–rigidity relation). `[conjecture]`** Within a declared repertoire, transcript class, edit graph, and target family, larger contextual assembly distance tends to accompany greater perturbational rigidity. A preregistered family showing no association, or a stable reversed order, refutes the conjecture on that declared domain.

For C2, coherence and identifiability are separate premises. A **single-battery comparison** uses one preregistered finite instrument \(F^\circ\), or losslessly transported copies of it, both to score local edits and to define the global matching fibres. For a finite reconstruction class \(\mathcal H_R\), put

\[
V_{F^\circ}(a)=
\{h\in\mathcal H_R:h\text{ matches }a\text{ on }F^\circ\}/\!\approx.
\]

Reverse inclusion of these fibres is the pinning preorder; a preregistered cardinality order may be used when fibres are incomparable. Let \(\rho_{F^\circ}(a)\) be the full local perturbation-response profile, not merely its scalar rigidity.

**C2-P1 (Single-battery coherence). `[protocol]`** Every local-rigidity and pinning comparison uses \(F^\circ\) or a witnessed lossless transport fixed before outcomes are inspected. A comparison that changes the battery, drops a distinguishing demand, or uses a lossy post-outcome transport refutes C2-P1 for that run.

**C2-P2 (Explicit identifiability geometry). `[protocol]`** Before held-out transmission outcomes are observed, the study supplies a non-degenerate, answer-free map \(\iota\) from the declared chart coordinates and \(\rho_{F^\circ}(a)\) to a predicted pinning rank or fibre class. The map may be specified analytically or fixed on a calibration split, but source identifiers, held-out answers, and target-specific decoders may not be inputs. Failure to construct such a map, leakage of those forbidden inputs, or collapse to one predicted rank refutes C2-P2 for that domain.

**Conjecture C2 (Field geometry–transmission relation). `[conjecture]`** On held-out members of a declared reconstruction domain satisfying C2-P1 and C2-P2, the preregistered predictions of \(\iota\) meet the declared pinning-order agreement threshold and predict transmission-fidelity order under matched closed budgets. Failure to meet either threshold, or a stable reversed order, refutes C2 on that domain. A5 has no inferential edge to C2-P1, C2-P2, or the field conjecture.

A finite **operative comparison frame** \(\Lambda\) selects the predecessor role marks and obligations or continuation conditions whose preservation is claimed, the admissible transports between cuts, and the gauge under which merely notational or carrier changes are ignored. The frame must include every predecessor mark invoked by the continuity or cumulative-growth claim and must be fixed before appraisal, or connected to the earlier frame by a witnessed lossless transport. Deleting a predecessor mark changes the claim. An empty frame can establish new acquisition from an empty predecessor cut, but cannot establish cumulative growth of a non-empty operative system.

An **abstract cumulative extension** across \(\mathcal K_t\to\mathcal K_{t+1}\) is an injective typed map

\[
J_\Lambda:\mathcal K_t\!\upharpoonright_\Lambda
\hookrightarrow
\mathcal K_{t+1}\!\upharpoonright_{\Lambda'}
\]

that preserves every selected role incidence, operative dependency, and undischarged obligation or continuation condition, while the terminal restricted state contains a non-gauge-trivial operative mark outside the image.

The promoted mark may be an account, constructor, criterion, problem generator, interface, or retained variant. The definition requires construction, consequential jeopardy, mediated award, and cumulative operative gain; it does not require represented explanation or a named rival.

An **explanatory Spark growth step** is a core growth step whose target is a first-class explanatory problem, whose promoted account is currently judged good on that problem, and whose displacement of any explanatory predecessor is mediated by a successor relation recovering the predecessor's genuine and apparent successes. Neither kind of step creates a truth certificate. Every current appraisal and standing remains reopenable under A-NJ.

A state has **creative capacity** when it can construct non-seed candidates, sustain consequential appraisal, promote under A5, and draw on an owned evaluated target source.

A state is **reflectively role-complete** when every role \(L,M,C,P,E\) is permission-open and has at least one reachable, non-gauge-trivial promotion path.

It has **universal reach** relative to an intended universe \(U\) when \(\operatorname{span}(L,M)=U\).

A **creative generator** has creative capacity and reflective role-completeness. A **universal creative generator** additionally has universal reach. A state is in a **growing regime** when a content guard blocks strict retreat on the declared growth coordinate. A **creative career** is a history that remains in the regime, continues to produce target-essential explanatory Spark growth steps without a terminal bound, and expands both its evaluated problem stream and a declared delivered-content coordinate without a fixed bound.

Capacity is architecture. Role-completeness is reachable revisability. Regime is current dynamics. Career is history. None entails the next.

**Corollary 8.1 (Finite behavioural underdetermination). `[elementary from B1]`** No finite input-output test entails creative capacity or reflective role-completeness in an unrestricted model class.

**Proof.** A terminating test has a finite interaction tree. A finite lookup transducer can reproduce every accepting branch while lacking construction provenance, problem ownership, appraisal, or role revision. ∎

Behaviour may refute an architectural attribution when it contradicts a claimed consequence. It cannot certify the architecture from a finite performance sample.

**Fragility Proposition. `[elementary]`** If a content-retreating award rule is constructible, an A5-admissible path promotes it into \(C\), and the scheduler selects that path, exit from the growing regime is reachable. Permission-openness of \(C\) alone is insufficient.

### S9. Concentrated consequences and constitutional barriers

The former N-series is not retained as a separate family of necessity results. Its pre-execution, retreat, and reach clauses were restatements of T3 and T9, T7, and T4; its role-obstruction clause followed immediately once an obstruction environment was defined; and its A5 clause merely reread the axiom. Those dependencies are now cited only where used.

**Diagonal cutoff note. `[conditional from L1, L2, A4, and an explicit delay construction]`** For any total computable cutoff assignment \(B(e)\), a fixed-point candidate can be constructed whose first declared success occurs after \(B(e)\). This blocks an infallible universal choice of safe finite cutoffs. It does not, without a physical resource model and a search policy, prove a theorem about energy, memory, or total expenditure.

**Proposition 9.1 (Constraint-barrier decomposition). `[elementary conditional]`** Let \(K\) be a non-vacuous decidable constraint on emitted outputs. If a violating output structure is constructible, a reachable admissible path installs the required criterion and interface changes, the scheduler may select it, and the output channel is available, then \(K\) is not guaranteed on every reachable trajectory.

A constitutional guarantee therefore requires at least one barrier in the path: reach restriction, channel closure, invariant inadmissibility, policy exclusion, or interface filtering. Those barriers spend different forms of freedom. No theorem says every output restraint destroys internal creativity.

**Prophet Conjecture. `[conjecture]`** For A5-compliant systems with reachable criterion revision, adversarially variable evaluated environments, the same observation channel as the predictor, and a fixed positive lead-time requirement, no predictor of bounded description length is uniformly correct across the environment class. ### S10. Composition and finite-coordinate bookkeeping

**Lemma 10.1 (Acyclic feed-line closure). `[elementary]`** An acyclic composition of systems whose targets are all supplied externally, and in which no evaluation trace returns to a constructor or target-selector, remains externally targeted at the composite boundary.

**Proof.** Topologically order the components. Every target entering a component is external or emitted by an earlier component. Without a feedback path from evaluation to construction or selection, no stage converts its own inadequacy into a later problem. ∎

**Proposition 10.2 (Emergent problem ownership). `[elementary]`** A cyclic composition of components that are not individually problem-owning can be problem-owning at the composite boundary when evaluation traces are retained and returned to a constructor or target-selector. Attribution is therefore boundary-relative and composition-sensitive.

**Lemma 10.3 (Finite-instrument bound). `[elementary]`** Against a fixed finite active instrument \(F\), the delivered-content coordinate is bounded by \(\operatorname{cont}(F)\). Unbounded growth of that coordinate requires new demands, expanded slices of a law-family, or changing instruments.

This bounds a measurement coordinate, not the explanatory reach or meaning of a theory. A single finite problem may motivate arbitrarily many accounts.

**Corollary 10.4 (Unbounded delivered-content bookkeeping). `[conditional from Lemma 10.3 and T7]`** Let a system use a fixed declared readout rule and suppose its delivered-content coordinate grows without a fixed bound. Then the active demand stream, the exposed slices of a law-family, or the instrument family is unbounded at some component or composite level; otherwise Lemma 10.3 supplies a fixed bound. Under all of T7's retreat-favouring hypotheses, sustained residence in the growing regime additionally requires a content guard or equivalent retreat blocker.

For a role \(r\), call an environment \(E_r\) an **\(r\)-obstruction environment** when every history that keeps \(r\) fixed has delivered content bounded by a declared constant. Robust unbounded growth in that particular environment requires a reachable revision path for \(r\) by immediate modus tollens. The substantive burden is the existence of \(E_r\); this corollary neither proves one for any role nor turns reachable revision into a guarantee of escape. A5 mediation follows only when A5 is separately assumed and is not a conclusion of observed growth.

**Fertility Conjecture. `[conjecture]`** Among matched-budget systems with A5-mediated promotion, universal reach, a content guard, and an unbounded evaluated problem source, policies that convert informative failure fringes into construction targets achieve greater long-run delivered content or lower assembly cost than library-local, promotion-shy, or directionless policies, except where the fringe carries no useful localization.

The bookkeeping corollary and Fertility Conjecture are not a biconditional. Architecture never certifies fertility.

### S11. Successor obligations and problem propagation

Let \(A^-\) and \(A^+\) be current represented explanatory accounts and let \(C(A^-,A^+)\) be a finite comparison record. Suppose it identifies a recovery region \(D_R\), on which \(A^+\) recovers genuine or apparent successes of \(A^-\), and a difference region \(D_\Delta\), on which \(A^-\) is defective, undefined, or displaced by additional content in \(A^+\).

**Proposition 11.1 (Successor-record compiler). `[conditional from A4 or an explicit compiler in the current move-set]`** Whenever the current repertoire can enact the displayed finite template compilation, it can construct at most the following non-duplicate candidate records:

\[
q_R:\text{ account for the represented recovery relation on }D_R,
\]

\[
q_\partial:\text{ account for the represented boundary between }D_R\text{ and }D_\Delta,
\]

\[
q_A:\text{ account for why }A^-\text{ appeared adequate under its earlier explicanda, tests, and frame}.
\]

For a candidate \(q\), define

\[
\operatorname{Obl}(q\mid C)
\iff
\operatorname{Depends}(q;D_R,D_\Delta,C)
\land
\neg\operatorname{Discharged}_C(q).
\]

The first conjunct says that varying at least one represented recovery, boundary, or apparent-success distinction changes the operative discharge condition after notational variation is quotiented. The second says the comparison package does not already contain a current good resolution and operative mastery of that condition.

The non-vacuous child set is

\[
Q_C^\star=\{q:\operatorname{Obl}(q\mid C)\}.
\]

No implication from non-empty \(D_R\) and \(D_\Delta\) to non-empty \(Q_C^\star\) is asserted.

**Proof.** The account identifiers, comparison record, region labels, and target templates are finite represented records. A4 supplies the finite compilation in the universal extension; outside that extension the same conclusion is conditional on the corresponding compiler being held in the current move-set. Non-vacuity, openness, and discharge require the separately defined witnesses. ∎

The compiler is diachronically downstream. It presupposes a represented successor comparison and cannot generate the first problem in a state containing no represented discrepancy, conflict, uncovered explicandum, failed expectation, failed transport, or inherited problem.

Let \(\overline{\mathfrak Q}^{\Phi}_{\mathrm{good}}\) be the classes that admit a physically possible good resolution, after quotienting by lossless target transports.

\Longrightarrow

\]

with an explicit initial provenance channel. A creative-capacity state with no inherited or constructed non-vacuous problem refutes this residue.

where the witness is a transported undischarged problem or a non-vacuous output of an explicit problem-construction channel. A selected resolution after which every problem is discharged and no new one is constructed refutes this residue.

### S12. Criticism geometry and acquisition

This module belongs to Spark because it concerns how a generator uses outcomes to narrow live explanatory alternatives. Its mathematical results are model-relative. Whether an abstract query is a physical test, whether its outcome can answer back, and whether its cost is closed are Poietic realization questions.

A finite exact acquisition model is

\[
\mathcal M=
(\mathcal H,\mathcal Q,(\mathcal Y_q)_{q\in\mathcal Q},\omega,
\mathcal A,c,\Pi).
\]

The finite set \(\mathcal H\) contains resolution classes after every equivalence the model treats as irrelevant. One class \(h^\star\) is the target. Each \(q\in\mathcal Q\) is an available criticism or test with finite outcome set \(\mathcal Y_q\), and

\[
\omega_q:\mathcal H\to\mathcal Y_q
\]

is its exact response map. For each non-empty version space \(V\subseteq\mathcal H\), \(\mathcal A(V)\) is the family of criticisms constructible at that stage. The positive closed cost \(c(q,V)\) includes construction, enactment, interpretation, update, and retention within the declared causal boundary. Policies in \(\Pi\) are deterministic adaptive rules and may stop only at a singleton.

After history \(h_m=((q_1,y_1),\ldots,(q_m,y_m))\), the version space is

\[
V(h_m)=\{h\in\mathcal H:\omega_{q_i}(h)=y_i\text{ for every }i\le m\}.
\]

Exact acquisition terminates at a singleton class, not necessarily at one representation.

For \(q\in\mathcal A(V)\), define

\[
V_{q,y}=\{h\in V:\omega_q(h)=y\},
\]

\[
\sigma(q,V)=
\max_{y:V_{q,y}\neq\varnothing}\frac{|V_{q,y}|}{|V|},
\]

\[
g(q,V)=-\log_2\sigma(q,V),
\qquad
\eta(q,V)=\frac{g(q,V)}{c(q,V)}.
\]

Assume the model presents the relevant query families effectively and that the suprema used below are attained; otherwise the usual \(\varepsilon\)-maximising form applies.

The exact Bellman value is

\[
\mathfrak C_{\mathcal M}(\{h\})=0,
\]

\[
\mathfrak C_{\mathcal M}(V)=
\min_{\substack{q\in\mathcal A(V)\\\sigma(q,V)<1}}
\left[c(q,V)+
\max_{y:V_{q,y}\neq\varnothing}
\mathfrak C_{\mathcal M}(V_{q,y})\right]
\]

for \(|V|>1\), with value \(+\infty\) when no separating test is available. The optimal worst-case closed cost is \(C^\star(\mathcal M)=\mathfrak C_{\mathcal M}(\mathcal H)\).

Let \(\mathcal V_{\mathcal M}\) be the reachable non-empty version spaces and define

\[
\underline\eta(\mathcal M)=
\inf_{\substack{V\in\mathcal V_{\mathcal M}\\|V|>1}}
\sup_{q\in\mathcal A(V)}\eta(q,V),
\]

\[
\overline\eta(\mathcal M)=
\sup_{\substack{V\in\mathcal V_{\mathcal M}\\|V|>1}}
\sup_{q\in\mathcal A(V)}\eta(q,V).
\]

**Imported Theorem 12.1 (Closed-cost criticism-compression bounds). `[imported]`** If \(|\mathcal H|=N>1\) and

\[
0<\underline\eta(\mathcal M)\le\overline\eta(\mathcal M)<\infty,
\]

then

\[
\frac{\log_2N}{\overline\eta(\mathcal M)}
\le
C^\star(\mathcal M)
\le
\frac{\log_2N}{\underline\eta(\mathcal M)}.
\]

**Proof.** For the lower bound, let an adversary choose a maximum-cardinality outcome cell after each query. The cardinality reductions telescope to \(\log_2N\), and each reduction is at most \(\overline\eta\) times its cost. For the upper bound, select a highest-efficiency available query at every live version space. Each query has cost at most its guaranteed log-contraction divided by \(\underline\eta\); the guaranteed contractions sum to no more than the telescoping actual reduction \(\log_2N\). Positive \(\underline\eta\) forces finite strict contraction. ∎

Call \(\mathcal M\) **\((\lambda,c_0)\)-criticism-separable** when every reachable non-singleton \(V\) has an available query satisfying

\[
\sigma(q,V)\le\lambda<1,
\qquad
c(q,V)\le c_0.
\]

**Corollary 12.2. `[elementary]`** Exact acquisition then needs at most

\[
\left\lceil\log_{1/\lambda}N\right\rceil
\]

queries and closed cost at most

\[
c_0\left\lceil\log_{1/\lambda}N\right\rceil.
\]

Outcome sensitivity alone is insufficient. Cheap acquisition depends on physically available partition structure whose worst outcome contracts the live class at favourable closed cost.

**Imported Proposition 12.3 (Bounded independent binary-noise lift). `[imported]`** Suppose logical tests are binary and repeatable, and every reachable non-singleton version space has an available logical test with \(\sigma(q,V)\le\lambda<1\). Conditional on the target and decoded history, repeated physical outcomes are independent and wrong with probability at most \(\nu<1/2\). Let each repetition cost at most \(c_{\mathrm{rep}}\), let majority aggregation cost \(c_{\mathrm{maj}}(r)\), and put

\[
m=\left\lceil\log_{1/\lambda}N\right\rceil.
\]

For failure tolerance \(0<\delta<1\), any odd

\[
r\ge\frac{2\ln(m/\delta)}{(1-2\nu)^2}
\]

supports majority decoding with total success probability at least \(1-\delta\) and cost at most

\[
m\bigl(r c_{\mathrm{rep}}+c_{\mathrm{maj}}(r)\bigr).
\]

This is physical noise bookkeeping, not a probability of theory truth or a support increment.

**Reduction Lemma 12.4 (Finite tolerance quotient). `[elementary]`** If a possibly infinite candidate class has a declared tolerance equivalence with finite quotient, every query outcome, availability relation, closed cost, and terminal condition descends to that quotient, then the quotient defines a finite exact model and Theorem 12.1 applies there. The existence and physical appropriateness of the quotient remain application obligations.

A factorization

\[
\mathcal H=\mathcal H_1\times\cdots\times\mathcal H_d
\]

is **diagnostically aligned** only when available criticisms separate one factor or bounded block while preserving the interpretation of the others, reachable version spaces preserve the product form, and resolved factors are retained. If factor \(i\) is \((\lambda_i,c_i)\)-separable, a factorwise policy has cost

\[
C\le
\sum_{i=1}^d
c_i\left\lceil\log_{1/\lambda_i}|\mathcal H_i|\right\rceil.
\]

Product notation without physically available aligned tests gives no such gain.

A learned sequence of models

\[
\mathcal M_0\xrightarrow{L_0}\mathcal M_1\xrightarrow{L_1}\cdots\xrightarrow{L_{m-1}}\mathcal M_m
\]

is legitimate only when each transport is available before use, preserves the target and outcome distinctions needed later, and includes its training, data, interpretation, and retention costs in the same resource account. A late representation with high criticism efficiency does not show that the original acquisition was cheap.

The remaining jump-constructor question is whether one fixed answer-generic architecture can construct, across a scaling family, physical application witnesses and stagewise models whose \(\underline\eta\) remains bounded away from zero or degrades slowly enough to beat whole-account search after the cost of constructing the representations, tests, operators, instruments, and retained background knowledge is included.

### P0. Jurisdiction and claim discipline

Poietic speaks only about physical attributes, task possibility, constructors, causal histories, information variables, retention, resources, and boundaries. It does not derive Deutschian epistemology from constructor theory. It provides the physical vocabulary and witness theorems required to apply Spark without smuggling physics into prose.

A mathematical statement can fail because its proof is wrong or a countermodel satisfies its premises and violates its conclusion. A physical application can fail because the required task is impossible under the actual law, a witness is absent, or a proposed realization map loses a load-bearing distinction. A Spark interpretation can fail even when every Poietic theorem remains true. These failure modes are kept separate.

### P1. Physical base

#### P1.1 Attributes, variables, tasks, and constructors

For a substrate \(S\), an **attribute** \(x\subseteq\Omega_S\) is a set of physical states sharing a specified property. A **variable** \(X=\{x_i\}_{i\in I}\) is a family of pairwise disjoint attributes of one substrate.

A **task** is a set of input–output attribute pairs

\[
\mathcal A=\bigcup_{i\in I}\{x_i\to y_i\}.
\]

The physical law \(\Phi\) partitions well-formed tasks into possible tasks \(\mathsf T^{\checkmark}_\Phi\) and impossible tasks \(\mathsf T^{\times}_\Phi\). A constructor for \(\mathcal A\) performs the task repeatedly while retaining the ability to perform it again. Sequential and parallel composition are admitted only when physical interfaces, substrates, and resource allocations match.

The current repertoire is the relation

\[
x\vdash_{\rho_\Sigma}\mathcal A,
\]

meaning that the ecology at cut \(\Sigma\) currently carries the constructors, resources, and operative conditions needed to perform \(\mathcal A\) from \(x\). Necessarily

\[
\rho_\Sigma\subseteq\mathsf T^{\checkmark}_\Phi.
\]

Physical possibility and present capability are distinct modal objects.

For a fixed finite physical architecture \(A\) with a common finite-program interface, define **constructional coverage** by

\[
\operatorname{UC}_\Phi(A)
\iff
\forall T\in\mathsf T^{\checkmark}_{\Phi,\mathrm{fin}},
\quad
A\text{ can, with a varying finite program and declared resources, construct a constructor for }T.
\]

A target-specific constructor may not be supplied as an untyped resource. \(\operatorname{UC}_\Phi\) is a physical coverage predicate, not a definition of personhood, explanation, or Spark universal reach. It is not a premise of R1–R5 and is invoked only when a constructional-universality claim is explicitly made.

#### P1.2 Information, joint presentations, and finite capacity

For a variable \(X\), let \(\operatorname{Perm}_\pi(X)\) be the task implementing permutation \(\pi\) on the attributes of \(X\). Given a receptive blank \(x_0\) on another medium, let

\[
\operatorname{Clone}_X=
\bigcup_{x\in X}\{(x,x_0)\to(x,x)\}.
\]

An **information variable** is a non-trivial variable for which every permutation task and the cloning task are possible. An information attribute is a member of an information variable. A substrate bearing one is an information medium. These are counterfactual task definitions. A constructor, task arrow, resource, causal history, institution, or capability does not become an information attribute merely because information configures it.

**Interoperability** is inherited as the principle that disjoint information media carrying \(X_1\) and \(X_2\) jointly carry the product information variable \(X_1\times X_2\). Finite iteration gives a product variable for any finite family on disjoint media.

A finite family of information-bearing faces is **jointly informational** on frame \(\Gamma\) when it is already carried on disjoint interoperable media or an explicit possible source-preserving encoding and decoding task maps every distinction used by the claim into one information variable \(X_\Gamma\). Joint informationality is local to the finite distinctions actually required.

The finite-capacity condition is

\[
I_{<\omega}:
\quad
\forall n<\omega,
\quad
\text{the requisite }n\text{ receptive instances of each declared information medium are preparable from generic resources.}
\]

This is arbitrarily large finite capacity, not an actually infinite store.

For a declared task class \(\mathcal C\), write

\[
\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathrm{reg}}(\mathcal C)
\]

when every finite regular network of possible tasks in \(\mathcal C\), with matched interfaces and explicitly allocated substrates, is possible. Extending the principle from information-processing tasks to maintenance, reconstruction, acquisition, or stage-gated transition networks requires the wider class to be declared and witnessed.

Information is not defined by topological discreteness. Task-digitality appears when a protected interface supports the correction structure proved below.

#### P1.3 Ecological state and live history

The physical state is

\[
\mathbb P_\Sigma=\langle\Phi;\rho_\Sigma,H_\Sigma\rangle.
\]

The realised history is an open event structure

\[
H_\Sigma=(E_\Sigma,\le_\Sigma,\ell_\Sigma,\partial_\Sigma).
\]

Its events are actual task tokens; \(\le_\Sigma\) records causal precedence; \(\ell_\Sigma\) records participating attributes, constructors, contexts, resources, and information channels; and \(\partial_\Sigma\) is the live frontier. The frontier may be a compatible family of local cuts rather than a preferred global simultaneity surface.

Write \(\operatorname{Inst}_\Sigma(x)\) when \(x\) is carried at the live frontier. History is not assumed to be an archive. An event may have occurred without leaving a reconstructible current record. A later Spark use of that event therefore requires a separate persistence witness under R2.

A historical witness occurs in \(H_\Sigma\). A current witness lies in \(\rho_\Sigma\) and, where bearer status matters, on \(\partial_\Sigma\). A possible witness belongs to \(\mathsf T^{\checkmark}_\Phi\). These modalities are never interchanged without an explicit transport.

#### P1.4 Frames and transports

A declared physical frame \(\Gamma\) contains the task boundary, environment, intervention family, comparison chart, resource account, temporal cut, encoding and interpretation interfaces, and every acquisition or realization model used by the claim. It does not certify its own adequacy.

A **frame transport**

\[
T:\Gamma\rightharpoonup\Gamma'
\]

is a partial structure-preserving map on the faces used by a claim. Its loss set contains undefined faces and every task distinction, composition, intervention, resource coordinate, interpretation, target equivalence, or boundary relation not preserved. Cross-frame inference is licensed only outside that loss set.

Replacing a frame after observing an outcome asks a new question unless an independently witnessed transport preserves the original claim.

#### P1.5 Intervention-sensitive dependence and resistance

A causal claim is indexed by

\[
\Gamma_I=(X,Y,C,\mathcal I,\simeq_Y),
\]

where \(X\) is a source variable, \(Y\) an outcome variable, \(C\) the exogenous background held fixed, \(\mathcal I\) a family of possible source interventions, and \(\simeq_Y\) the declared equivalence on outcome profiles.

Write

\[
X\rightsquigarrow_{\Gamma_I}Y
\]

when two possible interventions on distinct values of \(X\), with \(C\) preserved, produce non-equivalent outcome profiles. This relation is frame-relative and establishes neither unique pathway nor sufficiency.

A **resistant outcome interface** for a Spark appraisal consists of a candidate-configured source variable \(X_C\), a physical medium and context \(Z\), an outcome variable \(Y\), and a task network such that:

\[
(X_C,Z)\rightsquigarrow_{\Gamma_I}Y,
\]

at least two outcome distinctions used by the appraisal are physically possible somewhere in the declared intervention family, and the award gate receives \(Y\) causally downstream rather than setting \(Y\) directly. A policy may select the candidate, probe, or apparatus configuration. It may not count as testing if it can simply write the desired outcome without traversing the declared task network.

The resistant medium need not be external to a body or machine. A proof checker, simulator, consistency relation, game, error-correcting circuit, or experimental apparatus qualifies when its task structure satisfies the condition.

### P2. Constructor-theoretic knowledge-bearing continuation

#### P2.1 Information-value transport and retention profiles

Let \(X\) and \(X'\) be information variables. An **information-value transport**

\[
\theta:X\to X'
\]

is an invertible task-structure map preserving the information distinctions, permutation incidence, cloning incidence, and any interpretation used by the retention claim. Write

\[
k'\simeq_{\mathrm{Info}}k
\]

when such a transport maps \(k\) to \(k'\).

For an information attribute \(k\), environment \(E\), and intervention family \(\Delta\), a **retention circuit** is a historical, current, or possible causal diagram in which \(k\) configures a constructor or process whose effects contribute to a later instance \(k'\simeq_{\mathrm{Info}}k\).

The retention profile

\[
\mathsf R_{E,\Delta}(k)
\]

is the structured family of such circuits, marked by covered interventions, information transports, and causal routes. Coverage means that every intervention in \(\Delta\) has an appropriate retention circuit. It is not an observed frequency or probability.

#### P2.2 Copy-neutral causal comparison

A generic copy tail is a downstream copying or routing subdiagram whose triggering and task organisation are unchanged when the payload value is replaced. Contract each generic tail to a payload-preserving edge. Two profiles are **copy-neutral equivalent**, written \(\cong_{\mathrm{copy}}\), when the contracted causal diagrams are isomorphic while preserving intervention marks, non-copy configured effects, causal order, and recovered information distinctions.

A substitution \(k\mapsto\widetilde k\) is admissible when a possible intervention changes only the marked information value on the declared source boundary while preserving the exogenous background and generic copying machinery. If no such intervention is available, content-sensitive attribution is suspended on that frame.

Retention is **content-sensitive** when some non-copy route from \(k\) contributes to a later information-equivalent instance and, for some admissible \(\widetilde k\neq k\), the substituted profile is not copy-neutral equivalent to the credited profile, with the surviving difference intersecting that route.

#### P2.3 Physical knowledge predicate

An information attribute \(k\) is **Poietic knowledge on \((E,\Delta)\)** at a historical, current, or possible status when it has the corresponding bearer and retention witnesses, its retention profile covers \(\Delta\), and its retention is content-sensitive.

Current knowledge requires a current bearer and currently available retention disposition. Historical knowledge records a realised retention circuit. Possible knowledge is only knowledge-potential. Truth and explanatory goodness are not conjuncts of this physical predicate. A false, anti-rational, or purely hereditary item may qualify if its particular information recruits its own robust continuation.

A **knowledge-bearing ecology** contains at least one current Poietic knowledge attribute whose configured effects participate in the ecology's continuing operative organisation. This is the physical anchor used by Spark's bridge. It is not sufficient for Spark growth, good explanation, or personhood.

#### P2.4 Continuation, variation, constraint, and selection

A task boundary \(B\) is a finite or finitely specifiable family of task distinctions. A **continuation witness** from a section \(x\mid B\) to \(y\mid C\) consists of a historical, current, or possible process, a partial boundary transport, recovery of the predecessor task distinctions on the transport domain, and an intervention-sensitive causal inheritance witness. Reusing a label, genotype name, institutional title, or artistic signature is insufficient without recovered task structure and causal dependence.

A **variation** constructs a section, response, physical representation, interpretation-bearing record, or operator not equivalent to its source on the declared comparison boundary. It may be deterministic or stochastic, blind or represented, deliberate or accidental. Causal genesis supplies no epistemic merit.

A context is a **constraint** on a family of variants when it changes their transported continuation profiles relative to a declared baseline. No scalar fitness is required. A context may remove continuations, open them, alter recovery, or discriminate among variants.

**Selection** occurs when variants under a common constraint have different realised continuation profiles and that difference causally affects which variant information is later re-instantiated. Natural selection requires no represented criticism, explanation, problem, or Spark award relation.

A Spark application may identify a selection subgraph with consequential exposure and mediated retention only when W3 and the Spark identification map supply the required stage and role distinctions. The Poietic definition alone does not make blind elimination an epistemic criticism.

### P3. Capability structure and physical extension

For a physical boundary \(B\), let \(K_B(\Sigma)\) be the labelled structure generated by current knowledge bearers and the currently executable tasks, constructors, interpretations, transports, retention circuits, instruments, resources, and control organisations they configure. Its marked items are heterogeneous.

A **finite protection cut** \(\beta=(B,M)\) selects finitely many predecessor marks and every incidence, interpretation, transport, and retention distinction used by the preservation claim.

The **information skeleton** \(I_\beta(\Sigma)\) is the subdiagram containing the current information values in \(M\) and explicit information encodings of the labels, incidences, interpretations, and transport coordinates needed to identify those marks at the terminal cut. A description of a constructor is not the constructor.

The skeleton is **co-clonable** when it has a joint information presentation \(X_\beta\). It is **reconstructively complete** when there is a possible answer-generic task

\[
\operatorname{Rebuild}_\beta
=
\bigcup_{x\in X_\beta}
\{(x,g_\beta)\to(x,\widehat K_\beta(x))\},
\]

where \(g_\beta\) contains only declared generic resources and \(\widehat K_\beta(x)\) is marked-isomorphic on the cut to the capability structure configured by \(x\). The actual predecessor capability may not be hidden inside \(g_\beta\).

An embedding

\[
F:K_B(\Sigma)\hookrightarrow K_{B'}(\Sigma')
\]

is injective on marked items and preserves task incidence, current availability, causal inheritance, transport, interpretation, retention, and every comparison label. A **marked physical extension** exists when such an embedding preserves the predecessor cut and the terminal structure contains a current capability mark outside the image whose construction or activation is carried by the transition.

A marked physical extension is **knowledge-bearing at the new mark** when the new capability is configured for operative use by a current Poietic knowledge attribute, or by a losslessly linked information value, whose content-sensitive retention profile covers the declared perturbations and contributes to continued availability of that capability. This is an independently physical predicate. It supplies no verdict that the capability is explanatory, good, or epistemically progressive.

An **information-protection task** leaves one instance of the joint skeleton value intact and creates an information-equivalent instance on an allocated register. A **direct capability-protection witness** is a maintenance or isolation network covering every side effect of the extension task on the predecessor cut. A **reconstructive capability-protection witness** combines information protection, reconstructive completeness, generic resources, and a regular network that rebuilds a marked-isomorphic predecessor subsystem on compatible substrates.

#### Theorem P3.1. Full-code protected correction entails task-digital guard states

Let \(X=\{x_i\}_{i\in I}\) be an information variable and suppose **every attribute of \(X\) is protected by the same well-formed correction task**: for every \(j\in I\) the task has the identity face \(x_j\to x_j\), and for every corrected \(i\in I\) it has a non-trivial recovery face \(e_i\to x_i\), where \(e_i\) contains a state outside \(x_i\). Then each such recovery domain contains a state outside the whole code region \(|X|=\bigcup_jx_j\).

**Proof.** Fix a corrected \(i\). If every state in \(e_i\setminus x_i\) lay in \(|X|\), at least one lay in some disjoint code attribute \(x_j\) with \(j\neq i\). The same physical input state would then be required to remain in \(x_j\) by the identity face \(x_j\to x_j\) and to enter disjoint \(x_i\) by the recovery face \(e_i\to x_i\). The task would be ill formed. Hence \(e_i\setminus|X|\neq\varnothing\). ∎

The result establishes task-digital separation at the protected interface, not topological discreteness of the whole medium.

#### Proposition P3.2. Product-generic reproduction induces design dependence

Let \(P=\{p_i\}\) contain at least two disjoint heritable design attributes with whole-reproducer attributes \(R_p\). Suppose the same uncorrelated product input \(N\) and external context are used for every \(p\), no design-indexed tuner or non-elementary copier is hidden there, and

\[
(R_p,N)\to(R_p,R_p,W_p)
\]

is performed for every \(p\). Then the parent design projection intervention-depends on the offspring design projection under the frame that holds \(\Phi,N\), and the common apparatus fixed.

**Proof.** Replacing parent design \(p\) by distinct \(q\) while preserving the context requires offspring in disjoint \(R_p\) and \(R_q\), so the outcome profiles differ. ∎

This proves intervention-sensitive inheritance at the declared projection. It does not by itself establish modularity, a code, knowledge, or a unique internal channel.

#### Lemma P3.3. Composition of marked extensions

Marked structure-preserving embeddings compose whenever their boundary transports preserve every inherited distinction. A strict terminal mark of the second transition lies outside the composite image. ∎

#### Theorem P3.4. Finite protected copying of a co-clonable information skeleton

Let \(\beta\) be a finite cut whose skeleton has a joint information presentation \(X_\beta\). Assume \(I_{<\omega}\) for the required blanks and \(\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathrm{reg}}(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{info}})\) for finite information-processing networks. For every finite \(r\ge1\), the task

\[
\operatorname{Protect}^{I}_{\beta,r}
=
\bigcup_{x\in X_\beta}
\{(x,b_1,\ldots,b_r)\to(x,x_1,\ldots,x_r)\}
\]

is possible, where every \(x_j\simeq_{\mathrm{Info}}x\) and the source remains unchanged.

**Proof.** Cloning \(X_\beta\) is possible by the information-variable definition. Finite capacity supplies the blanks. A finite acyclic network of cloning tasks yields the requested instances, and regular composition makes the network possible. ∎

#### Proposition P3.5. Lifting information protection to capability protection

Let \(A\) be a possible task that terminates with a new current capability mark while retaining the declared common interface. A marked physical extension exists on cut \(\beta\) if either:

(a) a direct maintenance or isolation network covers every side effect of \(A\) on the predecessor cut and composes regularly with \(A\); or

(b) \(I_\beta(\Sigma)\) is co-clonable and reconstructively complete, finite capacity supplies the protected register and generic reconstruction resources, and information protection, rebuilding, and \(A\) form a regular network with no unallocated shared indispensable substrate.

**Proof.** The direct route preserves every predecessor mark while \(A\) creates the new mark. The reconstructive route copies the skeleton by Theorem P3.4, rebuilds a marked-isomorphic predecessor subsystem, and composes it with \(A\). Either route yields the required embedding plus a new terminal mark. ∎

#### Proposition P3.6. Classical information principles do not imply capability protection

There is a constructor-theoretic task model with unlimited finite classical information registers, interoperability, finite blank capacity, and regular information-processing composition in which two control values are Poietic knowledge, each associated capability is individually possible, and changing from one capability to the other is possible, yet no terminal state preserves both.

**Proof.** Add one conserved non-duplicable mastery substrate \(U\) with disjoint attributes \(u_0,u_1\). Let capability \(m_i\) require joint current attribute \((i,u_i)\), with the information value \(i\) selecting a content-sensitive maintenance circuit. Permit the transition \((0,u_0,g)\to(1,u_1)\). Any number of copies of bit \(0\) may be made, but no second \(U\) can be produced. A terminal witness for \(m_0\) requires \(u_0\); one for \(m_1\) requires disjoint \(u_1\) on the same unique substrate. Both cannot coexist. ∎

The proposition is the bridge's warning label. Record copying cannot discharge Spark R2 for an operative capability unless persistence, maintenance, or reconstruction is separately witnessed.


### P4. The Spark realization package

Fix a finite Spark cut \(\mathcal K_{\le t}\), a physical ecology \(\mathbb P_\Sigma\), and a frame \(\Gamma\). A **Poietic realization package** is

\[
\mathscr W_\Gamma=
(W_1,W_2,W_3,W_4,W_5;W_0,W_U),
\]

where W0 is required for a knowledge-growth attribution and WU is required only for the universal diagonal extension.

A tuple counts as one realization package only when its witnesses are **jointly coherent** on every shared bearer, task token, causal edge, resource label, interpretation, temporal cut, and boundary port used by the claim. No load-bearing distinction may lie in the loss set of a transport between witness frames. This coherence condition is definitional, not a consequence of listing W0–W5 side by side.

**W0, role-coupled physical knowledge anchor. `[physical witness]`** The operative organization identified with the Spark system is configured or maintained by at least one current Poietic knowledge attribute. For every role write claimed as a knowledge-growth step, the terminal promoted content, or a losslessly linked control value that identifies and configures its operative use, has a current content-sensitive retention circuit covering the declared perturbation family. An unrelated self-maintaining bit elsewhere in the ecology does not discharge W0, and W0 does not identify every Spark record as Poietic knowledge.

**W1, extensible record family. `[physical witness]`** For every finite set \(D\subset\mathbb S\) of Spark records used at the cut, closed under the quotation, pairing, projection, role-tag, and outcome-decoding operations actually invoked there, the ecology supplies a jointly informational variable \(X_D\), an injective encoding

\[
\eta_D:D\hookrightarrow X_D,
\]

and possible finite tasks implementing equality, the invoked encodings and decodings, finite copying, and registration of the corresponding event tokens. Larger finite cuts admit compatible extensions of the presentation or a lossless transport to a larger one.

W1 requires only arbitrarily extensible finite realization, not one actually infinite physical register.

**W2, dependency and role persistence. `[physical witness]`** For every Spark dependency edge from a record's availability to its later use, \(H_\Sigma\) contains a causal transport path preserving the encoded distinction to the use event. Every currently held role occupant whose operative semantics matter to the claim has both a persistent identifier or provenance record and a current physical bearer, task capability, or causal organization linked to that record by an operative interpretation witness. The capability is carried continuously, maintained against the declared perturbations, or reconstructibly recovered before use. Copying the identifier alone is insufficient. If A1i or deterministic replay is claimed, every event needed for replay additionally has a current archival bearer and operative decoder.

**W3, stage-separated role write. `[physical witness]`** Every physical role mutation identified with a non-seed Spark promotion has a finite causal subgraph with distinguished construction, consequential exposure, outcome-registration, award, and role-write task tokens. Every causal path that carries answer-bearing or occupant-determining content from held, seed, target, or external raw inputs into the new role occupant passes through a construction gate. Typed permission, criterion, and scheduling control paths may enter only their corresponding award or role-write gates and may not supply the occupant's answer-bearing content. Every causal path from the constructed candidate content to role write passes through consequential exposure, outcome registration, and award; every award-relevant path passes through at least one resistant outcome interface; and external syntax can enter an event or raw-record register without a direct content path to an operative role. Whenever the role write records tentative refutation, a physical comparison subgraph carries the rival, defect, common-explicanda, and recovery records that the Spark identification map uses to evaluate its tentative-refutation predicate. Poietic does not itself judge that predicate.

**W4, closed resource account. `[physical witness]`** For every completed transition claimed at the active Spark cut, the terminal-relevant physical causal contributors have a declared finite aggregate \(r_\Gamma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{fin}}\) in an ordered commutative resource monoid \((\mathcal R,+,\preceq)\). A tokenized account must have finite support or a separately witnessed finite-summation rule, and every charged task token has a resource label participating in the aggregate. The frame supplies a monotone finite coding or projection

\[
\lambda_\Gamma:\mathcal R_{\mathrm{fin}}\to\mathbb N^k
\]

used by Spark budgets. Every external constructor, prepared blank, instrument, memory operation, training process, maintenance route, energy or matter input, and interpretation process on which the transition depends crosses a typed boundary port and is included in the account.

**W5, causal boundary closure. `[physical witness]`** The declared boundary is a causal cut. Every cross-boundary edge relevant to the claim is typed as target or record input, resource or energy input, constructor service, observation, output, or control intervention. Every operative role mutation has all causal parents inside the boundary or represented by a typed recorded input. No unlabelled external path changes a role or supplies an answer-shaped constructor.

**WU, universal execution realization. `[optional physical witness]`** The ecology carries a current information-processing architecture and possible task family realizing universal interpretation, specialization, the finite recipes used by A4, and, when A1i is claimed, internal report extraction. WU is required only for the universal diagonal extension.

### P5. Discharge of R1–R5

#### Theorem P5.1. Record realization

\[
W_1\Longrightarrow R1.
\]

**Proof.** At the finite Spark cut, collect the finitely many record values and invoked record operations into \(D\). W1 supplies an injective physical presentation preserving equality and every used constructor or decoder. Interpret each abstract record by its encoded attribute and each record operation by the corresponding possible task. Distinct abstract values remain physically disjoint because \(\eta_D\) is injective. Hence all record distinctions used at the cut are realized. Compatible enlargement handles every later finite cut without requiring an infinite current medium. ∎

#### Theorem P5.2. Persistence realization

\[
W_2\Longrightarrow R2.
\]

**Proof.** Each later Spark use cites a dependency edge. W2 supplies a physical path from the earlier record bearer to the use event that preserves the encoded distinction, or reconstructs it before use with a witnessed transport. For every operative role use it also supplies the linked physical bearer, task capability, or causal organization, so current role standing is not inferred from an identifier alone. Continuous carriage, maintenance, or reconstruction realizes the required persistence. The stronger archival clause supplies internal replay exactly when claimed. Therefore every abstract reuse has the required physical persistence and no unclaimed full archive is inferred. ∎

#### Theorem P5.3. Transition realization

\[
W_3\Longrightarrow R3.
\]

If the Spark identification map labels the W3 stages as G1–G3 and verifies its raw-ingress no-bypass clause as G4, then every identified role mutation physically realizes A5.

**Proof.** W3 supplies distinct causal task tokens and their precedence, so construction, exposure, outcome registration, award, and role write cannot be collapsed by the realization map. Its content-path condition excludes a bypass from candidate or external syntax to the operative role while still permitting criteria, permissions, and scheduling to enter at their typed control gates. The resistant-interface condition makes at least one award-relevant verdict depend on a consequence rather than desired identity. The optional comparison subgraph supplies the additional two-account structure when tentative refutation is claimed. These are exactly the distinctions required by R3 and, under the stated identification, G1–G4. ∎

The theorem does not derive A5 as a law of physics. It proves that a physical architecture with W3 instantiates A5.

#### Theorem P5.4. Resource realization

\[
W_4\Longrightarrow R4.
\]

**Proof.** W4 supplies the finite terminal-relevant aggregate \(r_\Gamma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{fin}}\) for the completed transition, either by finite support or by its witnessed finite-summation rule. The projection \(\lambda_\Gamma(r_\Gamma)\) is therefore the finite budget coordinate used by Spark. Because every terminal-relevant external service and input crosses a typed priced port, no causal contributor on which the claimed transition depends is omitted. ∎

No scalar resource ordering is exported beyond the declared projection. Different frames may use time, energy, matter, memory, population, or a vector account.

#### Theorem P5.5. Boundary realization

\[
W_5\Longrightarrow R5.
\]

**Proof.** The causal-cut condition classifies every relevant cross-boundary parent or effect. The causal-closure condition ensures that an operative role mutation cannot acquire an unrecorded external parent. The declared frame therefore accounts for the target authors, stores, interfaces, resources, constructor services, and mutations used by the claim. ∎

#### Theorem P5.6. Joint realization theorem

Let \(\mathbb P_\Sigma\) supply a coherent package \(W_1\)–\(W_5\) for a finite Spark cut. Then there exists a typed realization map

\[
\mathcal R_{\Gamma,\Sigma}:\mathbb P_\Sigma\rightharpoonup\mathcal K_{\le t}
\]

satisfying R1–R5. If W3's stage subgraphs are identified with G1–G4, the realized role writes satisfy A5. If W0 also holds, every identified knowledge-growth role write is anchored in a role-coupled Poietic knowledge-bearing continuation. If WU holds, the structures in its domain realize A2–A4; together with W2's archival clause and internal report extraction, they realize A1i when that stronger claim is made.

**Proof.** Use Theorem P5.1 to map finite record values and operations. Use P5.2 to map dependency edges and current role occupancy. Use P5.3 to map transition relations and promotion certificates. Use P5.4 to map budget coordinates. Use P5.5 to fix the boundary and type every external causal edge. The package coherence condition makes the component maps agree on every shared face; any disagreement or transport loss blocks the joint map rather than being repaired by identification. W0 and WU add their stated predicates without altering R1–R5. ∎

This theorem is the promised discharge. It converts concrete physical witnesses into Spark's abstract realization conditions. Its hypotheses remain empirical or theory-relative when applied to an actual ecology.

### P6. Non-empty consistency model

**Theorem P6.1 (Finite interface satisfiability). `[elementary model construction]`** For every finitely presented A5-compliant Spark trace fragment, restricted to the record distinctions, role incidences, dependencies, and task tokens used at one finite cut, there exists a law-relative constructor-theoretic task model supplying a coherent W0–W5 package and realizing that fragment. If the fragment uses A2–A4, the model can additionally satisfy WU.

**Proof.** Let the constructed law-relative model permit arbitrarily many finite classical information registers and receptive blanks, while allocating only the finite family needed by the present trace fragment. Include permutation, cloning, pairing, projection, and finite logic tasks on those registers. For each Spark construction, evaluation, award, and role-write token, introduce a repeatable finite-state constructor with the same typed input and output incidence. Wire them in the trace's acyclic order and place a gate before every role register. External inputs terminate only at raw-record registers. Let a separate environment register or checker task supply the outcome variable used by each consequential appraisal, and exclude any selector-to-outcome bypass.

Assign one consumable resource token to each task event and include every token source at a typed boundary port. The finite network then satisfies W1, W3, W4, and W5. Add identity-preserving storage or reconstruction routes along every later dependency edge to satisfy W2. Finally, let each role tag introduced by a trace token, or a losslessly linked control value, select the maintenance route that repairs and re-establishes that tag and its linked finite-state role capability under a declared perturbation family. Substituting the control value changes the maintenance route, so every role write claimed as knowledge growth terminates in a role-coupled current Poietic knowledge attribute and W0 is satisfied. If universal execution is required, add a classical universal interpreter and specialization constructors on the record medium, supplying WU. Every network is finite and regular in the constructed law-relative model. ∎

The theorem establishes type-level consistency and a non-empty interface class. It does not show that the actual laws of our world permit every such model, that an actual creator supplies the required boundary, or that A5 is physically necessary.

### P7. Physical extension obstructions

For a physical ecology \(\mathbb P_\Sigma\), finite predecessor cut \(\beta\), and declared extension task \(A\), the direct and reconstructive routes can fail at the following independently typed faces.

| Obstruction | Physical failure |
|---|---|
| \(O_{\mathrm{joint}}\) | The required information faces have no joint information presentation. |
| \(O_{\mathrm{rebuild}}\) | A copied skeleton does not rebuild a marked-isomorphic predecessor capability from declared generic resources. |
| \(O_{\mathrm{capacity}}\) | Required blanks, substrates, constructors, energy, matter, time, protected locations, or other allocated resources are unavailable. |
| \(O_{\mathrm{coupling}}\) | Protection, rebuilding, and extension require one indispensable non-generic substrate in incompatible states or fail the declared regular-composition interface. |
| \(O_{\mathrm{retention}}\) | Extension side effects fall outside the predecessor's maintenance or isolation family. |
| \(O_{\mathrm{execute}}\) | No possible physical task constructs the declared new capability mark under the frame. |

### P8. Limits of Poietic

Poietic does not derive A5, A-NJ, good explanation, problems, tentative refutation, operational award, role revision, creative capacity, or any Spark conditional consequence. It can only show that specified physical task and causal structures realize those relations under a typed map.

Poietic knowledge is not epistemic approval. It includes false and anti-rational self-retaining information. Spark appraisal is therefore an additional layer, not a synonym.

A physical history is not automatically an archive. W2 must be separately satisfied for every later reuse, and full replay requires its stronger archival clause.

Information redundancy does not imply capability redundancy. The one-substrate countermodel P3.6 prevents R2 from being discharged by copied syntax alone.

Task possibility is law-relative. A constructor-theoretic consistency model is not evidence that the actual world supplies the same constructors, resources, or open boundary. WU realizes universal interpretation and specialization on an information-processing architecture; it does not imply the stronger physical coverage predicate \(\operatorname{UC}_\Phi\).

The realization package is frame-relative. A poor encoding, intervention family, resource boundary, or system cut can manufacture or erase an apparent Spark realization. Transport loss blocks the inference rather than being repaired by prose.

### I6. Mandatory refutation programme

Countermodels are not a testing programme. Every field conjecture, bridge application, and residue-bearing capstone claim therefore carries a protocol with a scheduled occasion to fail.

A protocol record fixes before execution the claim and domain, seed and answer boundary, target family, repertoire, battery, frame, gauge, compared policies or architectures, W4-closed resource budget, primary observable, decision threshold, stopping rule, exclusions, and localization rule. Its verdict is one of **REFUTED on the declared domain**, **SURVIVED THIS CUT**, or **INCONCLUSIVE**. Survival is not support. Statistical error control may govern a finite operational decision, but it is not a probability of theory truth.

Every applicable protocol runs before the first field use of its claim, after any change to a claim-defining repertoire, battery, geometry, policy, realization map, or boundary, and at every preregistered scaling cut. An omitted or stale run means **untested**, not survived. All compared arms receive the same terminal-relevant closed resource account unless the protocol's purpose is to compare cost-to-target directly.

**F1, assembly–rigidity challenge. `[protocol for C1]`** Use a preregistered family of gauge-inequivalent targets, one transcript-generation rule, one repertoire accounting convention, one local edit graph, and matched construction and evaluation budgets. Measure contextual assembly distance and local delivery rigidity before examining their association. The run refutes C1 on the declared domain when the preregistered association threshold is missed or a stable reversed order appears. Repertoire changes, censoring of failed constructions, and post-outcome edit-graph changes invalidate rather than rescue the run.

**F2, single-battery geometry–transmission challenge. `[protocol for C2]`** Split the declared reconstruction family into calibration and held-out members. Fix \(F^\circ\), its lossless transports, the local profile, the identifiability map \(\iota\), pinning order, transmission task, fidelity measure, and matched closed budgets before held-out outcomes are observed. First test C2-P1 and C2-P2 separately. Only a run passing both reaches C2. Failure of held-out pinning-order or transmission-order prediction at the preregistered threshold refutes C2 on that domain; it cannot be blamed on A5.

**F3, failure-fringe policy tournament. `[protocol for the Fertility Conjecture]`** Compare failure-fringe targeting with library-local, promotion-shy, and directionless policies using the same seed, model architecture, target stream, available operators, appraisal battery, promotion constitution, and W4-closed budget. Predeclare either delivered content at fixed cost or cost to a fixed delivered-content target as the primary measure. Stratify cases in which the fringe contains no usable localization before the result is known. Matched-budget domination by a rival on the preregistered scaling family refutes the Fertility Conjecture on that family.

**F4, fixed-decoder reconstruction challenge. `[protocol for Conjecture R and E2]`** Freeze one answer-generic source-to-account decoder before exposing the scaling target family. A finite family cannot by itself establish or refute the unrestricted universal quantifier unless the class is exhaustively represented by a proved reduction.

**F5, bounded-prophet challenge. `[protocol for the Prophet Conjecture]`** Fix predictor description bound, positive lead time, observation channel, system class, and adversarial environment class. For a finite environment class, enumerate it exhaustively; for an open class, preregister the generator and scaling cuts. A bounded predictor that is uniformly correct on the entire declared class while every displayed condition holds refutes the conjecture on that class. Failure caused by a shorter lead time, extra observation channel, fixed criteria, or non-adversarial environment does not reach the conjecture.

**F6, residue stress test. `[protocol for the finite-prefix capstone]`** Before the base step, exhibit or fail \(\operatorname{CreativeCapacity}(\mathcal K_0)\) and \(R_{P,0}\) separately. At every successor cut, separately exhibit or fail \(R_{P,+}\), \(R_S\), and \(R_X^{\Phi}\) on the accumulated predecessor cut. No residue may be inferred from the others, from Conjecture O, or from the boxed capstone. A missing witness blocks the next prefix and records exactly which residue failed; the composition theorem remains intact but inapplicable.

**F7, bridge audit. `[protocol for B-Core and B-Universal]`** Select the candidate Poietic knowledge-bearing extension independently of the Spark fit. Predeclare the admissible boundary and frame family, then audit W0–W5, package coherence, the identification map, A5 no-bypass, and, where claimed, WU and A1i. Global refutation requires the stronger universal counterexample, not merely failure of one convenient cut.

### I8. Source alignment

The non-inductivist source appears in the formal grammar rather than as a derived computational discovery. A-NJ is an exposed constitutional stipulation from that authority. Compatible evidence is an explicandum or finite delivery event, never support. A lone adverse result creates a problem; it does not tentatively refute an explanation without a good rival. A successor must recover the predecessor's genuine and apparent successes over the transported domain. Goodness remains a criticisable conjunction of explanatory account, non-idleness, and hardness to vary, not a total decision procedure. Standing is reversible and no positive terminal acceptance state exists.

Constructor theory enters only below the interface. Tasks are possible or impossible relative to \(\Phi\); information is defined by counterfactual permutation and cloning tasks; and Poietic knowledge is content-sensitive self-retaining information. This predicate anchors the physical domain without turning physical persistence into explanatory merit.

The source commitments that problems are inevitable, problems are soluble, people are universal explainers, and explanatory creativity is open-ended remain exposed as \(R_{P,0}\), \(R_{P,+}\), \(R_S\), Conjecture O, B-Universal, and the finite-prefix premises. Neither conjunct derives the other, and this schema is not a theorem of either kernel.
